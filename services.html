Common questions

Is the treatment safe for children and pets?

We choose materials and methods for homes with families. For gel baits and bait stations there is usually no need to leave. For spraying, we'll tell you how long to keep children and pets out of the treated rooms.

Do I need to empty my kitchen?

For most cockroach and ant treatments, no. We use gel bait in cabinets and corners. If a spray is needed, we'll tell you in advance what to cover or move.

How long does a visit take?

An apartment usually takes one to two hours. Villas, restaurants and buildings take longer, and we'll give you a time after the inspection.

How often should I book?

Homes often do well with a visit every three months. Restaurants and food businesses usually need a monthly visit to stay compliant and pest-free.

Do you offer contracts for businesses?

Yes. We offer monthly and quarterly contracts with scheduled visits and a service report after each one.
