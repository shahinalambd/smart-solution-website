How a visit works

Four steps, the same for a one-bedroom flat or a full building.

  1. Inspect

    We find where pests enter, nest and feed, and show you what we found.

  2. Plan

    You get a written plan: the areas, the materials and the timing.

  3. Treat

    Technicians use the right method for the pest: spray, gel bait, injection or bait stations.

  4. Follow up

    We check the results and tell you how to keep pests from coming back.

Termites work quietly. We find them.

Termites hollow out door frames and wooden fittings from the inside, often long before any damage shows on the surface.

  • Inspection of frames, skirting and wooden fittings
  • Drill-and-inject treatment into affected wood and walls
  • Barrier treatment to stop new colonies getting in
  • Advice on repairs once the colony is gone
